## 1. Executive Summary

A rigorous audit of the 30 references in `05_Research_Paper/manuscript/04_literature_review_and_reference_register.csv` using official publisher databases and Crossref digital object identifier (DOI) resolution revealed multiple significant bibliographic errors:
1. **Four references had completely mismatched DOIs** that resolved to unrelated articles published in the same or different journals (e.g., Chinese provincial panel data, German energy scenarios, and intangible capital).
2. **One reference had an erroneous DOI typo** in the APA 7th citation string (Vivoda, 2010).
3. **Fourteen references possessed valid DOIs and correct authors, but contained approximate, working, or synthetic titles** rather than the official published article titles.
4. **One reference (Mallick & Agarwal, 2014) lacked a DOI and could not be verified** in official indexing databases.

This document records the exact nature of every error, provides the official publisher-verified correction or authentic replacement, and establishes an unambiguous audit trail.

## 3. Mandatory Distinction: Metadata Verification vs. Substantive Findings Check

In strict adherence to academic standards, this audit enforces a clear distinction between two levels of verification:
1. **Bibliographic Metadata Verification:**  
   All 29 retained references have been 100% verified against official publisher records and Crossref metadata APIs. DOIs resolve directly to the published manuscripts.
2. **Substantive Findings Verification:**  
   Where full texts are behind publisher paywalls (Elsevier, Springer, Wiley), verification is confirmed via official publisher abstracts, keywords, and published econometric specifications. No numerical claim in the manuscript may be attributed to a literature source unless explicitly confirmed in the accessible text.
