## 3. Boundary Disclosures and Preliminary Data Handling

### 3.1 Status of FY2024–25 and FY2025–26 Data
- **Current Panel Boundary:** The verified empirical core dataset terminates at **FY2023–24 (FY24)**, covering exactly 26 continuous financial years (April 1997 to March 2024).
- **Preliminary Flash Data Status:** MoPNG and PPAC have released monthly flash snapshots for partial FY25. However, these figures represent provisional monthly estimates subject to end-of-year audit reconciliation, stock revisions, and customs adjustments.
- **Methodological Decision:** In accordance with strict academic standards, preliminary FY25 monthly data are **excluded from the core 26-year econometric and LMDI panel**. They are reserved exclusively for qualitative commentary in the Discussion section (Section 5) regarding contemporary post-panel developments.

### 3.2 Private Refining Financial Margins (GRMs)
- **Scope Limit:** The research rigorously analyzes physical refinery crude processing (MMT), domestic product output (MMT), and national import expenditures (USD B and INR Cr).
- **Commercial Firm-Level Data:** Corporate-level Gross Refining Margins (GRMs in USD/bbl) for private refiners (Reliance Industries Limited and Nayara Energy) are reported in corporate quarterly investor presentations but are not part of the standardized MoPNG national administrative accounting registers.
- **Methodological Decision:** Firm-level commercial profitability is contextualized qualitatively in Section 5.2 to explain export refining behavior, while keeping all core statistical tables anchored in national administrative aggregates.
